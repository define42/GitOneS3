The gitone-cluster-identity ConfigMap is immutable and retained on uninstall.
Back it up. Changing shardCount, hash/canonicalization, path limits, or the S3
mapping requires a data migration; do not force an in-place Helm upgrade.

All ordinals share one ServiceAccount in this chart. Strict per-shard bucket
IAM requires an ordinal-aware credential injector or another workload topology.

The PodDisruptionBudget is disabled by default because every pod is the only
serving owner of one shard. Plan OnDelete rollout batches around that constraint.
